Frequently asked questions

I paid but the app still shows the upgrade screen.
Tap Restore Purchases (in PoseLens Settings or on the PoseLens Pro screen). Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy.
How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are billed by Apple, so you cancel them in your Apple Account, not in the app: open iOS Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. See Apple's instructions.
How do I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
The match score doesn't show up.
Pose matching needs to see at least three quarters of your body (head to mid-thigh). Step back, or prop your iPhone further away, until the outline fits. Good light helps.
How does Auto capture work?
When your match score reaches the threshold and you hold still for a moment, PoseLens takes the photo for you. Turn Auto on or off with the Auto button on the camera screen, and change the sensitivity (Relaxed, Normal, Strict) in Settings. Moving poses such as walking or jumping never stay still long enough, so use the shutter or the volume button for those.
What is Guided capture?
A step bar at the bottom of the camera — Distance, Best angle, Pose — shows one tip at a time: auto zoom frames you at the right size, then angle tips, then pose fixes. Tap a step to jump to it, or turn Guided capture off in Settings. In Solo the steps are Distance and Pose.
Can I copy a pose from my own photo?
Yes. In the library, tap From your photo and pick a photo with one person, whole body visible. PoseLens turns the pose into an outline for that session only; the photo is not saved or uploaded. You get 3 free uses, then it is included with PoseLens Pro.
What's free and what do I get with PoseLens Pro?
Free: 20 poses with Guided capture and Auto capture, plus 3 uses of From your photo. Pro: all 80 poses, unlimited From your photo, and new pose packs added in app updates. Choose Lifetime (pay once) or Yearly (renews automatically each year). There is no free trial and no weekly plan.
Where are my photos saved?
In your Photos library. PoseLens shows the photos taken in the current session. If you delete a photo from inside PoseLens, iOS asks for full Photos access the first time; PoseLens never asks otherwise and never browses your library. If you said no to Camera or Photos access, open iOS Settings → PoseLens and turn Camera on, or set Photos to Add Photos Only.
