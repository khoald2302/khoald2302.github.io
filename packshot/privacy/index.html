Privacy Policy

Packshot · Last updated October 3, 2026

This policy explains what information Packshot (“the app”, “we”) collects, why, and who it is shared with. It is written to match exactly what the app does.

Summary

  • No ads, no tracking. We do not show third-party ads, do not collect your advertising identifier (IDFA), and do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • AI features. Content you choose to submit is sent to Magic Hour AI, Inc. for processing — only after you agree on the in-app “Before we send your photo” screen.
  • No account, no server of our own. Your photos, results and history are stored only on your device. Your credit balance is also backed up to your own iCloud account so purchased credits are not lost.
  • We never sell your data.

2. What stays on your device

  • The photos you import, the cut-outs, white-background shots and AI scenes you create, and your history.
  • Captions and listing text — these are written on your device by Apple's on-device model and never leave it.
  • Your credit wallet, your AI-consent choice and your app settings.
  • Background removal, white backgrounds and label reading all run on your device.
  • Delete everything at any time with Settings → Delete All Data.

What we do not collect:

  • Your name, email address or any account — the app has no sign-in.
  • Your original photos — only the cut-out product image is sent, and only for AI Scenes after you agree.
  • Your advertising identifier (IDFA), precise location, contacts or browsing history.
  • Crash reports — the app does not include a crash-reporting SDK.

3. Device permissions

Packshot asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it. You can decline, and you can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings.

  • Camera — to shoot a product photo inside the app. The photo stays on your device.
  • Photos (add only) — to save your finished product shots to your photo library. Packshot cannot read your library; photos you import are picked by you with the system photo picker.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal information and do not share it with advertisers. We share data only with these service providers, each of which is bound by its own privacy policy and data-protection terms:

  • Magic Hour AI, Inc. — Creates the AI scene (the new background) around your product when you use AI Scenes. Receives: the cut-out image of your product (the original photo and its background never leave your device) and the text description of the scene you picked. Shared only with your in-app consent. Their privacy policy: magichour.ai/privacy-policy
  • Google Firebase (Remote Config) — Delivers app configuration needed to run AI Scenes. Not used for advertising or cross-app tracking. Receives: an app-instance identifier, app version, device model, OS version and locale that Firebase needs to deliver the configuration. Their privacy policy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — Measures how the app is used (for example which features are opened, whether a photo was saved, whether a purchase completed) so we can fix problems and improve Packshot. Not used for advertising or cross-app tracking. You can turn it off at any time in Settings → Legal & Privacy → Share Usage Data. Receives: usage events and their settings (never your photos and never text you type), an app-instance identifier, app version, device model, OS version, locale, approximate region derived from your IP address, purchase events (product and result), and — if you installed from an Apple Ads campaign — the campaign of that install. Their privacy policy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Apple (AdServices and AdAttributionKit) — Measures whether our ads work without tracking you. Apple tells us, once per install, whether the install came from an Apple Ads campaign. For installs from other ad networks, iOS itself sends the ad network an anonymous, aggregated conversion value (for example: onboarding finished, first photo saved, purchase) — the app never sees or shares who you are. Receives: an anonymous attribution token created by iOS (no advertising identifier), and a coarse conversion value that iOS sends on the app's behalf. Their privacy policy: www.apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Apple (iCloud Key-Value Storage) — Keeps a backup of your credit balance in your own iCloud account so purchased credits come back when you reinstall or change device. Receives: your credit balance and credit purchase/usage records — no photos. Their privacy policy: www.apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Legal requirements — we may disclose information if required by law, or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets.

5. How long we keep it

Data stored on your device stays there until you delete it or uninstall the app. We do not run our own servers and keep no copy of the content you submit. AI providers process requests to return a result to you and retain request data only as described in their own policies. Analytics and crash data are kept for the provider's standard retention period.

6. Legal bases

For AI features we rely on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. For analytics and crash diagnostics we rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and improving it. For purchases, we rely on performing our contract with you.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal information. In the app you can:

  • Withdraw AI consent in Settings → Legal & Privacy → Send Photos to AI → Turn Off. Background removal and white backgrounds keep working.
  • Turn off usage analytics and ad measurement in Settings → Legal & Privacy → Share Usage Data. Nothing else in the app changes.
  • Revoke Camera or Photos access in iOS Settings.
  • Delete your data in Settings → Delete All Data, or by deleting the app. Your credits are not affected.

For any other request, email khoald2302.ai@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Tracking

We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. We do not access your advertising identifier (IDFA), so the app never shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version, and material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
