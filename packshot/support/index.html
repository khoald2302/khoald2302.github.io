Frequently asked questions

Why does Packshot ask me to agree before creating an AI scene?
AI Scenes send the cut-out image of your product to Magic Hour AI to generate the new background. We ask first so you know exactly what is shared. If you tap No, thanks, nothing is sent, and background removal and white backgrounds keep working.
I reinstalled the app. Where are my credits?
Credit packs are one-time purchases, so the App Store cannot restore them. Your credits come back through iCloud when you use the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on. Tap Restore Purchases in Settings to sync. If they still don't appear, email us with your Apple purchase receipt.
An AI scene failed. Did I lose credits?
No. Credits are held while a scene is being generated and are only charged when you receive a result. If the generation fails, the held credits are released back to your wallet.
How do I delete my data?
Use Settings → Delete All Data in the app, or delete the app. Your photos and history live only on your device, so this removes them completely.
