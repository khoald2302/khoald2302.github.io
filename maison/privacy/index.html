Privacy Policy

Maison: Clock & Photo Widgets · Last updated November 12, 2026

This policy explains what information Maison: Clock & Photo Widgets (“the app”, “we”) collects, why, and who it is shared with. It is written to match exactly what the app does.

Summary

  • No ads, no tracking. We do not show third-party ads, do not collect your advertising identifier (IDFA), and do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No account, no server of our own. Your widget designs, the photos you add to widgets, your saved wallpapers and your color themes are stored only on your iPhone, in storage shared between Maison and its widgets. Nothing you design is uploaded.
  • We never sell your data.

2. What stays on your device

  • Your widget designs — styles, fonts, finishes, countdown dates and the text you write.
  • Photos you add to widgets. Photos you pick with Apple's photo picker are copied into Maison's storage on your iPhone, resized to at most 1000 pixels.
  • Wallpapers you save to the Library, and the color themes taken from them.
  • The screenshots you choose for the Clear finish. Maison cuts out only the area behind each widget and keeps that on your iPhone.
  • Your settings — onboarding progress, tips you have seen and your weather city.
  • Delete a design in Library (touch and hold it → Delete), or delete the app to remove everything.

What we do not collect:

  • Your name, email address or any account — the app has no sign-in.
  • Your photos, the text you add or anything you design.
  • Your calendar events and the song playing in Apple Music. Maison reads them on your iPhone only to draw your widgets; they are not part of the usage events above.
  • Your photo library — Maison never browses it. You choose photos with Apple's photo picker, and the app only asks to add wallpapers to your library.
  • Your advertising identifier (IDFA), contacts or browsing history. Maison shows no ads, does not track you across apps and never shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt.

3. Device permissions

Maison: Clock & Photo Widgets asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it. You can decline, and you can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings.

  • Photos (add only) — to save the wallpapers and Live Photos you choose to your photo library so you can set them as your background. Maison cannot read your library with this permission.
  • Location (while using the app) — to show local weather on your widgets. You can add a city by name instead.
  • Calendars — to show today's events on your Day Board widget. Events stay on your iPhone.
  • Apple Music — to show the song playing in Apple Music on your Music widget.
  • Notifications — only if you add the Daily Greeting control, to send your greeting at the time you pick.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal information and do not share it with advertisers. We share data only with these service providers, each of which is bound by its own privacy policy and data-protection terms:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase — Measures how the app is used (for example whether a widget was saved or placed on the Home Screen, whether a set was applied, whether the Pro screen was opened and a purchase completed, and which errors people run into) so we can fix problems and improve Maison. Not used for advertising or cross-app tracking. Receives: a small set of usage events and their settings (never your photos, your text, your calendar or anything you design), an app-instance identifier, app version, device model, OS version, locale, approximate region derived from your IP address, and purchase events (product and result). Their privacy policy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Apple Weather (WeatherKit) — Provides the forecast shown on your Weather, Day Board and Greetings widgets. Receives: the location you choose for weather — your approximate current location if you allow it, or the city you add. It is sent to Apple to fetch the forecast and is not linked to you.. Their privacy policy: www.apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Apple (App Store purchases) — Processes your purchase or subscription payment and verifies that you own Maison Pro. Receives: your purchase receipt, handled by Apple. We never see your payment details, Apple Account or name.. Their privacy policy: www.apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Legal requirements — we may disclose information if required by law, or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets.

5. How long we keep it

Data stored on your device stays there until you delete it or uninstall the app. We do not run our own servers and keep no copy of the content you submit. Analytics and crash data are kept for the provider's standard retention period.

6. Legal bases

For analytics and crash diagnostics we rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and improving it. For purchases, we rely on performing our contract with you.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal information. In the app you can:

  • Revoke any permission in *iOS Settings → Maison*. Widgets that need it show a short note instead.
  • Delete your data by deleting designs in Library, or by deleting the app — everything Maison stored is on your iPhone.
  • Cancel a subscription in *iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions*, or from Manage subscription in Maison Settings.

For any other request, email khoald2302.ai@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Tracking

We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. We do not access your advertising identifier (IDFA), so the app never shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version, and material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
