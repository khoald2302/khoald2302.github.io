Frequently asked questions

I paid but the app still shows the upgrade screen.
Tap Restore Purchases (in Settings → Restore purchases, and at the bottom of the Maison Pro screen). Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy.
How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are billed by Apple, so you cancel them in your Apple Account, not in the app: open iOS Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. See Apple's instructions.
How do I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
How do I add a widget to my Home Screen?
Save a design in Maison, then touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, search for Maison, pick a size and tap Add Widget. Maison shows these steps with an animation under Settings → How to add widgets.
My widget shows a different design. How do I change it?
Touch and hold the widget, tap Edit Widget and choose the design you want.
Why is there no second hand on the clock?
iOS updates widgets about once a minute, so no widget app can show a moving second hand. Maison's clocks show hours and minutes.
Which music apps work with the Music widget?
Apple Music only. iOS does not let widgets read what other music apps are playing.
How does the Clear finish work?
Clear places a cut-out of your wallpaper behind the widget so it blends in. Calibrate once in Light mode and once in Dark mode from Settings → Calibrate Clear. iOS still draws a thin glass edge around every widget, so the widget is very close to invisible but not fully.
Why can't Maison set my wallpaper for me?
iOS doesn't let apps change your wallpaper. Maison saves it to Photos for you; then open it in Photos, tap Share → Use as Wallpaper.
Is Maison free?
Yes. Clock, photo, countdown and text widgets, wallpapers and sets are free, with no ads. Maison Pro adds more finishes, typefaces, wallpapers and sets. Choose monthly, yearly (with a 7-day free trial) or a one-time Lifetime purchase. Anything free today stays free.
How do I restore my purchase on a new device?
Open *Settings → Restore purchases*, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought with.
How do I delete my data?
Delete designs in Library, or delete the app. Everything lives only on your iPhone, so this removes it completely.
