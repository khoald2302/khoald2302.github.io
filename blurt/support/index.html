Frequently asked questions

I paid but the app still shows the upgrade screen.
Tap Restore Purchases (on the Blurt Pro screen (Restore Purchases, at the bottom)). Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy.
How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are billed by Apple, so you cancel them in your Apple Account, not in the app: open iOS Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. See Apple's instructions.
How do I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
Blurt asks me to agree before it sorts my notes. Why?
Sorting sends the text of your note to OpenAI. We ask first so you know exactly what is shared. If you decline, Blurt still records and keeps your transcript, it just does not sort it.
Recording does not turn into text.
Open iOS Settings → Blurt and turn on Microphone and Speech Recognition. Some languages, Vietnamese among them, need Server recognition turned on in Blurt's Settings.
Blurt misheard a name or a word.
Speak one language per recording and check the underlined words on the review screen before saving. You can edit the transcript of any note and Blurt sorts it again.
How many AI sorts are free?
Your first 3 notes are sorted for free. Blurt Pro unlocks unlimited sorting.
How do I delete my data?
Delete a note in the app to remove it and everything sorted from it, or delete the app. Your notes live only on your device.
