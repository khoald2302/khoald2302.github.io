Privacy Policy

Blurt: Voice To-Do List · Last updated October 16, 2026

This policy explains what information Blurt: Voice To-Do List (“the app”, “we”) collects, why, and who it is shared with. It is written to match exactly what the app does.

Summary

  • No ads, no tracking. We do not show third-party ads, do not collect your advertising identifier (IDFA), and do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • AI features. Content you choose to submit is sent to OpenAI for processing — only after you agree on the in-app “Your thoughts stay yours.” screen.
  • No account, no server of our own. Your notes, tasks, ideas, events and settings are stored only on your device.
  • We never sell your data.

2. What stays on your device

  • Your notes and their transcripts, and the tasks, ideas and events sorted from them.
  • The people, projects and places linked between your notes.
  • Your settings, your AI-processing choice and your free AI-sort count.
  • Delete a note and everything sorted from it at any time; deleting the app removes all of it.

What we do not collect:

  • Your name, email address or any account — the app has no sign-in.
  • Your voice recordings — audio is turned into text and is never saved by the app.
  • Your advertising identifier (IDFA), precise location, contacts or photos.
  • Usage analytics or crash reports — the app includes no analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

3. Device permissions

Blurt: Voice To-Do List asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it. You can decline, and you can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings.

  • Microphone and Speech Recognition — to turn what you say into text. Transcription runs on your device where iOS supports it; with Server recognition turned on, Apple's speech service transcribes the languages iOS cannot do offline.
  • Reminders — to create reminders for the dated tasks you approve. Blurt never changes or deletes a reminder it did not create.
  • Calendars — to add the events you approve and to let you pick which calendar they go to. Blurt never changes or deletes an event it did not create.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal information and do not share it with advertisers. We share data only with these service providers, each of which is bound by its own privacy policy and data-protection terms:

  • OpenAI — Sorts a note into tasks, ideas and events, gives it a title and short summary, and reads out dates, places and the people, projects and places it mentions. Receives: the text of your note (transcribed from your voice or typed), today's date and your time zone, and the names of people, projects and places you have mentioned in earlier notes. Shared only with your in-app consent. Their privacy policy: openai.com/policies/privacy-policy
  • Apple (Speech Recognition) — Turns your voice into text for a language iOS cannot transcribe offline, only if you turn on Server recognition. Receives: the audio of your voice while you are recording. Their privacy policy: www.apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Google Firebase (Remote Config) — Delivers a small app configuration value the AI feature needs. No analytics, no crash reporting, never used for advertising or tracking. Receives: an app-instance identifier, app version, OS version and locale, sent when the configuration is fetched. Their privacy policy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Legal requirements — we may disclose information if required by law, or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets.

5. How long we keep it

Data stored on your device stays there until you delete it or uninstall the app. We do not run our own servers and keep no copy of the content you submit. AI providers process requests to return a result to you and retain request data only as described in their own policies.

6. Legal bases

For AI features we rely on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. For purchases, we rely on performing our contract with you.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal information. In the app you can:

  • Withdraw AI consent in Settings → Privacy → AI processing by declining. Nothing is sent to OpenAI after that.
  • Turn Server recognition off in Settings to keep all transcription on your device.
  • Revoke Microphone, Speech Recognition, Reminders or Calendars access in iOS Settings.
  • Delete notes in the app, or delete the app to remove everything.

For any other request, email khoald2302.ai@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Tracking

We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. We do not access your advertising identifier (IDFA), so the app never shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version, and material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
