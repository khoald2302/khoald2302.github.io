Privacy Policy

Snugroom: Cozy Focus Timer · Last updated November 18, 2026

This policy explains what information Snugroom: Cozy Focus Timer (“the app”, “we”) collects, why, and who it is shared with. It is written to match exactly what the app does.

Summary

  • No ads, no tracking. We do not show third-party ads, do not collect your advertising identifier (IDFA), and do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No account, no server of our own. Your focus sessions, labels, to-dos, sound mixes, coins, room and settings stay on your iPhone or iPad. If you turn on iCloud sync, they are kept in your own private iCloud, which we cannot read. The only data that reaches us is anonymous usage measurement and crash reports, which never include any of that content.
  • We never sell your data.

2. What stays on your device

  • Your focus sessions and statistics, labels, to-dos, saved sound mixes, coins, room decorations, chosen companion and settings.
  • Sounds you import yourself. They are copied into the app on your device and never uploaded to us.
  • The apps you choose to rest during focus. iOS gives Snugroom only opaque tokens, so the app never learns their names.
  • Delete everything at any time with Settings → Delete all data, or by deleting the app. Your Snugroom+ purchase is not affected and can be restored.

What we do not collect:

  • Your name, email address or any account, because Snugroom has no sign-in and no server of its own.
  • Your label names, to-dos, mix names, imported sounds or anything else you type or create.
  • Your advertising identifier (IDFA), precise location, contacts, photos or microphone. Snugroom never shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • The apps you block, your browsing history or how long you use any other app.
  • Payment details, which are handled by Apple.

3. Device permissions

Snugroom: Cozy Focus Timer asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it. You can decline, and you can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings.

  • Notifications — to tell you when a focus or break phase ends and to remind you 2 days before a free trial ends. Notifications are scheduled on your device.
  • Approximate location — only if you turn on Real weather, to look up the weather outside your window through Apple Weather. Never precise location, never stored.
  • Screen Time (Family Controls) — only if you turn on App blocking, to rest the apps you choose while a focus session runs. Everything is handled on your device by Apple's Screen Time; you can revoke access at any time in iOS Settings → Screen Time.
  • Files — only when you import your own sound, to copy the file you pick into the app.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal information and do not share it with advertisers. We share data only with these service providers, each of which is bound by its own privacy policy and data-protection terms:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase — Measures which features are used and which errors happen (for example whether onboarding was finished, a focus session was started or completed, or the Snugroom+ screen was opened) so we can fix problems and improve Snugroom. Not used for advertising or cross-app tracking, and it does not read your advertising identifier. Receives: usage events with fixed values such as session length in minutes, where a session was started from and fixed error codes (never your label names, to-dos, mix names or imported sounds), the Snugroom+ plan chosen and whether a purchase succeeded, an app-instance identifier, app version, device model, OS version, locale and approximate region derived from your IP address. Their privacy policy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Firebase Crashlytics — Collects crash reports and app-performance information so we can find and fix bugs. Receives: crash stack traces, app version, device model, OS version, fixed error codes and an installation identifier. Never the content you create in the app. Their privacy policy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Apple (App Store purchases) — Processes the Snugroom+ yearly subscription and the one-time Lifetime purchase and verifies that you own them. Receives: your purchase and subscription transactions, handled by Apple. We never see your payment details, Apple Account or name. Their privacy policy: www.apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Apple Weather (WeatherKit) — Only if you turn on Real weather: looks up the current weather so the window in your room can show rain, snow or a clear sky. Receives: your approximate location, sent to Apple for the weather lookup. Not stored by us. Their privacy policy: www.apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Apple iCloud (CloudKit) — Only if you turn on iCloud sync: keeps your sessions, labels, to-dos, mixes, coins, room and imported sounds in sync across your devices. Receives: the app content listed above, stored in your own private iCloud database. We cannot access it. Their privacy policy: www.apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Legal requirements — we may disclose information if required by law, or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets.

5. How long we keep it

Data stored on your device stays there until you delete it or uninstall the app. We do not run our own servers and keep no copy of the content you submit. Analytics and crash data are kept for the provider's standard retention period.

6. Legal bases

For analytics and crash diagnostics we rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and improving it. For purchases, we rely on performing our contract with you.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal information. In the app you can:

  • Delete all your data in Settings → Delete all data, or by deleting the app.
  • Turn off Real weather, iCloud sync or App blocking at any time in Settings.
  • Revoke Location, Notifications or Screen Time access in iOS Settings.

For any other request, email khoald2302.ai@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Tracking

We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. We do not access your advertising identifier (IDFA), so the app never shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version, and material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
