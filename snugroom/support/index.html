Frequently asked questions

I paid but the app still shows the upgrade screen.
Tap Restore Purchases (in Settings → Restore Purchases, or at the bottom of the Snugroom+ screen). Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy.
How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are billed by Apple, so you cancel them in your Apple Account, not in the app: open iOS Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. See Apple's instructions.
How do I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
Can I listen to Spotify or Apple Music while Snugroom plays sounds?
Yes. Play with other audio is on by default in Settings, so your rain or fireplace plays on top of your own music. Turn it off if you want Snugroom to pause other audio.
Does the timer keep running when I lock my phone?
Yes. The timer and sounds keep going in the background, and the current phase shows on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. You get a notification when each phase ends if notifications are allowed.
How do I earn coins?
Every real minute of focus earns coins, plus a small bonus for finishing a session. Spend them in the Shop on lamps, plants, shelves, rugs, wall art and curtains, then place them in your room.
What is Honest mode?
An optional setting. If you leave Snugroom during a focus session, those minutes don't count toward coins or stats. Locking your screen is fine. Nothing is ever taken away.
How do I cancel the free trial or subscription?
Open iOS Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Snugroom and tap Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or period ends. Snugroom also reminds you 2 days before a free trial ends.
I bought Snugroom+ but it's not unlocked.
Tap Restore Purchases in Settings or at the bottom of the Snugroom+ screen, signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy. Lifetime and active subscriptions are restored.
What happens to my room if Snugroom+ ends?
Nothing is removed. Rooms, decor, companions and saved mixes you already have stay in your room; you just can't add new Snugroom+ items until you subscribe again.
How do I sync between my iPhone and iPad?
Turn on iCloud sync in Settings on both devices, signed in to the same iCloud account. Your data stays in your own private iCloud.
App blocking isn't working.
Open Settings → App blocking, check that Screen Time access is granted and that you chose at least one app. Apps are rested only while a focus session runs, and pausing a session lifts the block.
