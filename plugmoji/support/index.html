Frequently asked questions

I paid but the app still shows the upgrade screen.
Tap Restore Purchases (on the Plugmoji Pro screen, or in Settings → Restore Purchases). Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy.
How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are billed by Apple, so you cancel them in your Apple Account, not in the app: open iOS Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. See Apple's instructions.
How do I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
Can Plugmoji change the iPhone's battery icon?
No. iOS does not let any app change the system battery icon. Plugmoji makes Lock Screen wallpapers where your character hangs right under the real battery icon, plus charging screens, widgets and Live Activities that show your character with your battery level.
How do I set a Plugmoji wallpaper?
Save the wallpaper to Photos in the app, then in the Photos app open it, tap Share → Use as Wallpaper. The in-app Setup Guide shows each step. If your character looks misaligned, turn off Perspective Zoom when setting the wallpaper.
Why doesn't the charging screen appear when the app is closed?
iOS only lets an app show its charging screen when it is open. To open it automatically when you plug in, create a Shortcuts automation ("When charger is connected → open Plugmoji"). The Setup Guide in the app walks you through it.
Why doesn't my Live Photo wallpaper move?
Make sure the Live button is on when you set the wallpaper, then touch and hold the Lock Screen. Live wallpapers do not move on the Home Screen or in Low Power Mode.
What does Plugmoji Pro include?
All characters, battery styles, wallpapers, charging screens and widget templates, animated characters on the Lock Screen, and characters made from your own photos. Many characters and templates are free for everyone.
How do I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
How do I delete my data?
Use Delete my data in Settings, or delete the app. Your designs live only on your iPhone, so deleting the app removes them. To stop anonymous usage statistics and crash reports, turn off Share usage statistics in Settings → Privacy & data.
