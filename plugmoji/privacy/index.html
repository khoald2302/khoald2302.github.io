Privacy Policy

Plugmoji · Last updated November 11, 2026

This policy explains what information Plugmoji (“the app”, “we”) collects, why, and who it is shared with. It is written to match exactly what the app does.

Summary

  • No ads, no tracking. We do not show third-party ads, do not collect your advertising identifier (IDFA), and do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No account, no server of our own. Your designs, wallpapers, widgets, charging screens, the characters you make from your own photos and your settings are stored only on your iPhone (and in your iPhone's own iCloud backup if you use one). Characters are cut out from your photos on your iPhone — your photos are never uploaded. The only data that leaves your iPhone is anonymous usage measurement and crash reports, which you can turn off at any time.
  • We never sell your data.

2. What stays on your device

  • The wallpapers, charging screens and widgets you design, and the characters and battery styles you choose.
  • Characters you create from your own photos (the cut-out image is saved in the app's private storage on your iPhone so widgets and Live Activities can show it).
  • Your settings, sounds and charging history.
  • Delete everything with Delete my data in Settings, or by deleting the app.

What we do not collect:

  • Your name, email address or any account, because Plugmoji has no sign-in.
  • Your photos. When you make a character from a photo, the background is removed on your iPhone with Apple's on-device Vision framework; the photo is never uploaded.
  • Your advertising identifier (IDFA), precise location, contacts or browsing history. Plugmoji shows no ads, does not track you across apps and never shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • Payment details — Plugmoji Pro is bought through Apple, and we never see your card, Apple Account or name.

3. Device permissions

Plugmoji asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it. You can decline, and you can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings.

  • Photos (add only) — to save the wallpapers and Live Photos you create to your Photos library. Plugmoji cannot read your library with this permission. When you make a character from a photo, you pick that one photo with the system photo picker, which gives the app only the photo you choose.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal information and do not share it with advertisers. We share data only with these service providers, each of which is bound by its own privacy policy and data-protection terms:

  • Google Firebase (Google Analytics for Firebase, Crashlytics) — Measures how the app is used (for example whether onboarding was finished, a wallpaper was saved or the Pro screen was opened) and reports crashes, so we can fix problems and improve Plugmoji. Not used for advertising or cross-app tracking. You can turn it off at any time in Settings → Privacy & data → Share usage statistics. Receives: usage events and their settings (never your photos, wallpapers or designs), an app-instance identifier, app version, device model, OS version, locale, approximate region derived from your IP address, and crash logs. Their privacy policy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Apple (App Store purchases) — Processes the Plugmoji Pro subscription or one-time purchase and verifies that you own it. Receives: your purchase receipt, handled by Apple. We never see your payment details, Apple Account or name. Their privacy policy: www.apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Legal requirements — we may disclose information if required by law, or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets.

5. How long we keep it

Data stored on your device stays there until you delete it or uninstall the app. We do not run our own servers and keep no copy of the content you submit. Analytics and crash data are kept for the provider's standard retention period.

6. Legal bases

For analytics and crash diagnostics we rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and improving it. For purchases, we rely on performing our contract with you.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal information. In the app you can:

  • Turn off usage statistics and crash reports in Settings → Privacy & data → Share usage statistics. Nothing else in the app changes.
  • Turn off Live Activities for Plugmoji in iOS Settings → Plugmoji at any time.
  • Delete a design or a character you made in the app at any time.
  • Delete all your data with Settings → Delete my data, or by deleting the app.

For any other request, email khoald2302.ai@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Tracking

We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. We do not access your advertising identifier (IDFA), so the app never shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version, and material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
