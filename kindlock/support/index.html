Frequently asked questions

I paid but the app still shows the upgrade screen.
Tap Restore Purchases (in Settings → Subscription, or at the bottom of the Kindlock Pro screen). Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy.
How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are billed by Apple, so you cancel them in your Apple Account, not in the app: open iOS Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. See Apple's instructions.
How do I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
How do I turn Kindlock off?
To pause one plan, open Plans and switch it off (a Strict plan asks for its unlock first). To turn off every block, open Settings → Protection status → Reset all blocks. To remove access entirely, open iOS Settings → Screen Time. Kindlock always tells you honestly when protection is off.
An app is still blocked after I deleted Kindlock or a plan.
iOS sometimes keeps a block after an app is removed. Try these in order: reinstall Kindlock; open Settings → Protection status → Reset all blocks; turn off Screen Time access for Kindlock in iOS Settings → Screen Time; restart your iPhone. Your plans stay saved through the second step.
I can't turn on Screen Time access.
Check iOS Settings → Screen Time → Content & Privacy Restrictions and make sure nothing blocks changes to Screen Time. Another app, or a device-management profile from work or school, may be managing Screen Time. On a child account in Family Sharing, a parent has to allow it. Then open Kindlock and tap Try again.
I lost my unlock key, code or tag.
On the unlock screen choose I can't use my key. After a 30-minute wait you can unlock and set up a new one. You can also turn off Screen Time access in iOS Settings at any time.
Why do the numbers in Insights differ from iOS Settings?
Insights are drawn by Apple's own Screen Time report. Differences can appear, for example when Share Across Devices is on or when the report last refreshed. Tap Reload under the report.
What is Gentle mode?
A plan that never blocks completely. Each time you open an app in the group, Kindlock asks you to take a short pause first, then you choose to open it for a set time or not now.
Does Kindlock Pro work with Family Sharing?
Yes. Annual and Lifetime Pro can be shared with up to 5 family members through Apple Family Sharing. Monthly cannot be shared.
What happens to my plans if Pro ends?
Nothing is deleted. Your groups, plans and history stay on your iPhone; you choose which plans stay on within the free limits.
