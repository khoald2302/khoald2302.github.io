Privacy Policy

Kindlock: Screen Time Limit · Last updated November 12, 2026

This policy explains what information Kindlock: Screen Time Limit (“the app”, “we”) collects, why, and who it is shared with. It is written to match exactly what the app does.

Summary

  • No ads, no tracking. We do not show third-party ads, do not collect your advertising identifier (IDFA), and do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No account, no server of our own. Your Screen Time data never leaves your device: the apps and websites you block, your plans, schedules, limits, focus sessions and history stay on your iPhone. The only data that leaves it is anonymous usage measurement, which never includes any of that.
  • We never sell your data.

2. What stays on your device

  • The apps, categories and websites you choose to block. iOS gives Kindlock only opaque tokens for apps and categories, so the app itself never learns their names.
  • Your app groups, schedules, daily limits, focus sessions, Gentle and Strict settings, and challenges.
  • Your plan history: days you kept a plan, focus sessions finished, extra-time requests and the reasons you typed.
  • A one-way hash of your unlock key, NFC tag or partner code, never the key itself.
  • Screen time numbers in Insights are drawn by Apple's own Screen Time report on your iPhone; Kindlock cannot read them as numbers and never sends them anywhere.
  • Delete everything at any time with Settings → Delete all data, or by deleting the app.

What we do not collect:

  • Your name, email address or any account, because Kindlock has no sign-in and no server of its own.
  • The apps, websites and categories you block, your browsing history or how long you use any app.
  • Your advertising identifier (IDFA), precise location, contacts or photos. Kindlock never shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • Camera images: when you scan an unlock key, nothing is recorded or saved.
  • Crash reports, because the app includes no crash-reporting SDK.
  • Payment details, which are handled by Apple.

3. Device permissions

Kindlock: Screen Time Limit asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it. You can decline, and you can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings.

  • Screen Time (Family Controls) — to block the apps and websites you choose, run your schedules and limits, and show your screen time report. Everything is processed on your iPhone by Apple's Screen Time; you can revoke access at any time in iOS Settings → Screen Time.
  • Notifications — to tell you before a schedule starts or a limit runs out, to let you ask for more time, and to remind you before a free trial ends. Notifications are scheduled on your iPhone.
  • Camera — only if you choose an unlock key for Strict mode, to scan that key. Nothing is recorded or saved; only a one-way hash of the key is kept on your iPhone.
  • NFC — only if you choose an NFC tag as your unlock method, to write and read your own tag.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal information and do not share it with advertisers. We share data only with these service providers, each of which is bound by its own privacy policy and data-protection terms:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase — Measures which features are used and which errors happen (for example whether onboarding was finished, a plan was saved, a focus session was started or the Pro screen was opened) so we can fix problems and improve Kindlock. Not used for advertising or cross-app tracking, and it does not read your advertising identifier. Receives: usage events and fixed error codes (never the apps or websites you block, your group or plan names, your schedules, your screen time, your unlock key or code, or the reasons you give), the Pro plan chosen and whether a purchase succeeded, an app-instance identifier, app version, device model, OS version, locale and approximate region derived from your IP address. Their privacy policy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Apple (App Store purchases) — Processes Kindlock Pro subscriptions and the one-time Lifetime purchase, including Family Sharing, and verifies that you own them. Receives: your purchase and subscription transactions, handled by Apple. We never see your payment details, Apple Account or name. Their privacy policy: www.apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Legal requirements — we may disclose information if required by law, or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets.

5. How long we keep it

Data stored on your device stays there until you delete it or uninstall the app. We do not run our own servers and keep no copy of the content you submit. Analytics and crash data are kept for the provider's standard retention period.

6. Legal bases

For analytics and crash diagnostics we rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and improving it. For purchases, we rely on performing our contract with you.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal information. In the app you can:

  • Delete all your data in Settings → Delete all data, or by deleting the app.
  • Turn off every block in Settings → Protection status → Reset all blocks.
  • Revoke Screen Time access in iOS Settings → Screen Time, or revoke Notifications or Camera access in iOS Settings.

For any other request, email khoald2302.ai@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Tracking

We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. We do not access your advertising identifier (IDFA), so the app never shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.
