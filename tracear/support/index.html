Frequently asked questions

I paid but the app still shows the upgrade screen.
Tap Restore Purchases (in TraceAR Settings or on the TraceAR Pro screen). Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy.
How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are billed by Apple, so you cancel them in your Apple Account, not in the app: open iOS Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. See Apple's instructions.
How do I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
How do I set up my iPhone to trace?
Put your paper on a table and hold your iPhone 20–40 cm above it, on a stand, a glass or a stack of books, with the camera pointing down. Open a template, then pinch, drag and rotate the outline until it sits where you want it on the page. Tap Lock so a stray touch doesn't move it, then draw along the faint lines with your own pencil.
I don't have a stand. Can I still trace?
Yes. Use Freeze to hold the current camera frame still, or turn on Light Box from the More menu: the screen shows the outline on bright white, you tap Lock touch and lay thin paper directly on your iPhone to trace it like a light table. Light Box does not need the camera.
How do I turn my own photo into something to trace?
Tap Import photo on Home (or Paste if you copied an image, or share a photo to Trace with TraceAR from Photos or Safari). Crop it, then pick a style such as Outline or Bold Lines and a subject preset. Everything is processed on your iPhone.
Can I trace a name or a message?
Yes. Tap Text on Home, or Create your own in the Letters category, type up to 40 characters, choose a font and Outline or Filled, then tap Trace this.
What's free and what do I get with TraceAR Pro?
Free: tracing with flash, grid, Freeze and Light Box, free templates and lessons, photo-to-sketch with the basic styles, and the free fonts. Pro unlocks all templates and lessons, every sketch style and background removal, line colors and edge highlight, Strobe, paper calibration and all Text Trace fonts. Choose Weekly, Monthly or Yearly (renews automatically; Yearly starts with a 3-day free trial) or Lifetime (pay once).
I said no to camera access.
Open iOS Settings → TraceAR and turn Camera on. Until then you can still trace with Light Box, which does not use the camera.
