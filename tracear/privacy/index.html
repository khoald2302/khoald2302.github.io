Privacy Policy

TraceAR: AR Drawing Sketch · Last updated October 31, 2026

This policy explains what information TraceAR: AR Drawing Sketch (“the app”, “we”) collects, why, and who it is shared with. It is written to match exactly what the app does.

Summary

  • No ads, no tracking. We do not show third-party ads, do not collect your advertising identifier (IDFA), and do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No account, no server of our own. Your projects, sketches, finished drawings, recordings, favorites and settings are stored only on your iPhone. Turning photos into line art, tracing over the camera, Light Box and Text Trace all run on your device; your photos, drawings and the text you type are never uploaded.
  • We never sell your data.

2. What stays on your device

  • Projects you trace: the template or photo, the line-art sketch made from it, its position, opacity and drawing time.
  • A photo you import with the system photo picker, paste, or share to TraceAR from another app (the Trace with TraceAR share extension only places the image in TraceAR's own storage on your iPhone).
  • Photos of your finished drawings and time-lapse recordings — only when you tap Finish or Record. They are copied to your Photos library or shared only when you choose Save to Photos or Share.
  • The text, font and style you choose in Text Trace.
  • Favorites, collections, lesson progress, daily challenge streaks and your settings. The home-screen widget reads your streak from the same on-device storage.
  • Deleting the app removes everything TraceAR stored.

What we do not collect:

  • Your name, email address or any account — the app has no sign-in.
  • Your photos, camera frames, drawings, recordings, or anything you type in Text Trace.
  • Your photo library — TraceAR uses Apple's photo picker, which only gives the app the photo you pick, and add-only access when you save a drawing to Photos.
  • Your advertising identifier (IDFA), precise location, contacts or browsing history. TraceAR does not track you across apps and never shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • Crash reports — the app does not include a crash-reporting SDK.

3. Device permissions

TraceAR: AR Drawing Sketch asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it. You can decline, and you can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings.

  • Camera — to show your template over the paper so you can trace it, and to photograph or record your drawing when you tap Finish or Record. The camera runs only on the trace screen; frames are processed on your iPhone and never uploaded.
  • Photos (add only) — to save your finished drawing or recording to your Photos library when you choose Save to Photos. TraceAR cannot read your library.
  • Notifications — only if you turn on the Daily Reminder, to remind you of the daily challenge at the time you choose. Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal information and do not share it with advertisers. We share data only with these service providers, each of which is bound by its own privacy policy and data-protection terms:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase — Measures how the app is used (for example whether onboarding was finished, whether a tracing session was started or a drawing saved, whether the Pro screen was opened and a purchase completed, and which errors people run into) so we can fix problems and improve TraceAR. Not used for advertising or cross-app tracking. Receives: a small set of usage events and their settings (never your photos, camera frames, drawings, recordings or the text you type), an app-instance identifier, app version, device model, OS version, locale, approximate region derived from your IP address, and purchase events (product and result). Their privacy policy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Apple (App Store purchases) — Processes your purchase or subscription payment and verifies that you own TraceAR Pro. Receives: your purchase receipt, handled by Apple. We never see your payment details, Apple Account or name.. Their privacy policy: www.apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Legal requirements — we may disclose information if required by law, or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets.

5. How long we keep it

Data stored on your device stays there until you delete it or uninstall the app. We do not run our own servers and keep no copy of the content you submit. Analytics and crash data are kept for the provider's standard retention period.

6. Legal bases

For analytics and crash diagnostics we rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and improving it. For purchases, we rely on performing our contract with you.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal information. In the app you can:

  • Revoke Camera, Photos or Notifications access in iOS Settings → TraceAR.
  • Delete a project or drawing from My Art, or delete all your data by deleting the app — everything TraceAR stored is on your iPhone.
  • Cancel a subscription in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or from Manage Subscription in TraceAR Settings. Pro Lifetime is a one-time purchase and never renews.

For any other request, email khoald2302.ai@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Tracking

We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. We do not access your advertising identifier (IDFA), so the app never shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version, and material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
