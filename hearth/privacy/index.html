Privacy Policy

Hearth: Period Tracker Calendar · Last updated October 11, 2026

This policy explains what information Hearth: Period Tracker Calendar (“the app”, “we”) collects, why, and who it is shared with. It is written to match exactly what the app does.

Summary

  • No ads, no tracking. We do not show third-party ads, do not collect your advertising identifier (IDFA), and do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • Apple Health. Health data stays on your device and is never sent to our AI providers, our servers, or any third party.
  • No account, no server of our own. Your periods, symptoms, moods and notes are stored only on your iPhone. Hearth has no account and no server. The only data that leaves your iPhone is anonymous usage measurement — never your cycle or health data — which you can turn off at any time.
  • We never sell your data.

2. What stays on your device

  • The periods you log (start and end dates, flow), and your symptoms, moods and notes.
  • Your cycle settings (cycle and period length), reminder choices and app preferences.
  • Cycle predictions, which are calculated on your iPhone from your own history.
  • If you turn on Apple Health sync, menstrual flow you sync with Apple Health; periods imported from Health become part of your Hearth history on this iPhone.
  • Delete everything at any time with Settings → Privacy & Data → Delete All Data.

What we do not collect:

  • Your name, email address or any account, because Hearth has no sign-in.
  • Your cycle, symptom, mood and note logs and your Apple Health data, which never leave your iPhone unless you export or back them up yourself.
  • Your advertising identifier (IDFA), location, contacts or browsing history. Hearth does not track you across apps and never shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • Crash reports, because the app includes no crash-reporting SDK.
  • Payment details — Hearth 1.0 is free and has no in-app purchases.

3. Device permissions

Hearth: Period Tracker Calendar asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it. You can decline, and you can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings.

  • Face ID / Touch ID — to lock the app, only if you turn on the app lock. The check happens on your iPhone; Hearth never receives your biometric data.
  • Notifications — to remind you before your next period or to log your day, only if you turn reminders on. Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone and are worded discreetly.
  • Apple Health — to read and write menstrual flow only, and only if you turn on Apple Health sync. The data stays on your iPhone and is never sent to us, to analytics or to iCloud.

4. Apple Health (HealthKit)

If you connect Apple Health, the app uses it only to power in-app features:

  • Read: menstrual flow — used on your iPhone to add periods you logged in Apple Health to your Hearth history — only if you turn on Apple Health sync.
  • Write: menstrual flow (periods you log in Hearth) — saved to Apple Health with your permission. When you shorten or delete a period in Hearth, Hearth removes only the samples it wrote itself; it never deletes data from other apps. Deleting all data in Hearth does not delete anything in Apple Health.

Health data is kept on your device by Apple Health. We never send it to our AI providers, analytics providers, our servers, advertisers or anyone else, and we never use it for advertising or data mining.

You can review or revoke access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Hearth.

5. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal information and do not share it with advertisers. We share data only with these service providers, each of which is bound by its own privacy policy and data-protection terms:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase — Measures how the app is used (for example whether onboarding was finished, which screens are opened, whether you saved a log today) so we can fix problems and improve Hearth. Events never contain dates, cycle phases, symptoms, moods, notes or predictions. Not used for advertising or cross-app tracking. You can turn it off at any time in Settings → Share Usage Data. Receives: a small set of usage events and their settings (never cycle or health data), an app-instance identifier, app version, device model, OS version, locale and approximate region derived from your IP address. Their privacy policy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Legal requirements — we may disclose information if required by law, or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets.

6. How long we keep it

Data stored on your device stays there until you delete it or uninstall the app. We do not run our own servers and keep no copy of the content you submit. Analytics and crash data are kept for the provider's standard retention period.

7. Legal bases

For Apple Health we rely on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. For analytics and crash diagnostics we rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and improving it.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal information. In the app you can:

  • Turn off usage analytics in Settings → Share Usage Data. Nothing else in the app changes.
  • Delete all your data in Settings → Privacy & Data → Delete All Data, or by deleting the app. This does not delete anything in Apple Health.
  • Export your data for your doctor as a one-page PDF or a CSV file in Settings → Privacy & Data.
  • Turn off Apple Health sync, reminders or the Face ID lock in the app, or revoke Health, Notifications or Face ID access in iOS Settings.

For any other request, email khoald2302.ai@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Tracking

We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. We do not access your advertising identifier (IDFA), so the app never shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt.

10. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.
