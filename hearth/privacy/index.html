Privacy Policy

Hearth: Period Tracker Calendar · Last updated October 4, 2026

This policy explains what information Hearth: Period Tracker Calendar (“the app”, “we”) collects, why, and who it is shared with. It is written to match exactly what the app does.

Summary

  • No ads, no tracking. We do not show third-party ads, do not collect your advertising identifier (IDFA), and do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No account, no server of our own. Your periods, symptoms, moods and notes are stored only on your iPhone. Hearth has no account and no server, and it never sends your data anywhere.
  • We never sell your data.

1. What leaves your device

Nothing. The app makes no network requests of its own and sends no data to us or to any third party. Data leaves your iPhone only when you choose to share it, for example when you export a summary for your doctor or create a backup file and send it with the share sheet. Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store; we never see your payment details.

2. What stays on your device

  • The periods you log (start and end dates, flow), and your symptoms, moods and notes.
  • Your cycle settings (cycle and period length), reminder choices and app preferences.
  • Cycle predictions, which are calculated on your iPhone from your own history.
  • Whether you have unlocked the full version, checked on your device with Apple's StoreKit.
  • Delete everything at any time with Settings → Privacy & Data → Delete All Data.

What we do not collect:

  • Your name, email address or any account, because Hearth has no sign-in.
  • Your cycle, symptom and mood logs, which never leave your iPhone unless you export them yourself.
  • Your advertising identifier (IDFA), location, contacts or browsing history.
  • Analytics or crash reports, because the app includes no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDK.
  • Payment details, because purchases are processed by Apple.

3. Device permissions

Hearth: Period Tracker Calendar asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it. You can decline, and you can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings.

  • Face ID / Touch ID — to lock the app, only if you turn on the app lock. The check happens on your iPhone; Hearth never receives your biometric data.
  • Notifications — to remind you before your next period or to log your day, only if you turn reminders on. Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone and are worded discreetly.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal information and do not share it with advertisers. We use no third-party service providers, so there is no one we share your data with. We do not receive your data ourselves either; a file you export or back up goes only where you choose to send it.

5. How long we keep it

Data stored on your device stays there until you delete it or uninstall the app. We do not run our own servers and keep no copy of the content you submit.

6. Legal bases

For purchases, we rely on performing our contract with you.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal information. In the app you can:

  • Delete all your data in Settings → Privacy & Data → Delete All Data, or by deleting the app.
  • Export your data for your doctor as a one-page PDF or a CSV file in Settings → Privacy & Data.
  • Turn off reminders or the Face ID lock in the app, or revoke Notifications or Face ID access in iOS Settings.

For any other request, email khoald2302.ai@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Tracking

We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. We do not access your advertising identifier (IDFA), so the app never shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version, and material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
