Frequently asked questions

I paid but the app still shows the upgrade screen.
Tap Restore Purchases (in Settings → Nightpage Pro → Restore Purchases, and at the bottom of the Nightpage Pro screen). Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy.
How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are billed by Apple, so you cancel them in your Apple Account, not in the app: open iOS Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. See Apple's instructions.
How do I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
Are my books or scans uploaded anywhere?
No. Your PDFs, scanned pages, recognised text and reading positions stay on your device. Text recognition and read-aloud run on your device.
Read-aloud skipped a line or misread a word on a scanned page. What can I do?
Pages marked Check page may have missed a line. Tap the badge and retake that page with the book pressed flat in good light. Retakes do not use your free scan pages.
How do I get a better reading voice?
Nightpage uses the voices installed on your device. Download an Enhanced or Premium voice in *iOS Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices*, then pick it in *Settings → Voice*.
What does Nightpage Pro include?
Unlimited scanning, unlimited listening and no ads. Choose a yearly subscription or a one-time Lifetime purchase. Both work with Family Sharing.
How do I restore my purchase on a new device?
Open *Settings → Nightpage Pro → Restore Purchases*, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought with.
How do I delete my data?
Delete a book in the Library, or delete the app. Everything lives only on your device, so this removes it completely.
