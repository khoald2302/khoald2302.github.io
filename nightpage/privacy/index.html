Privacy Policy

Nightpage: PDF Book Reader · Last updated October 22, 2026

This policy explains what information Nightpage: PDF Book Reader (“the app”, “we”) collects, why, and who it is shared with. It is written to match exactly what the app does.

Summary

  • Tracking. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob in the Library — never on the reading or listening screens. If you choose Allow in the App Tracking Transparency prompt, Google AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to personalise ads and measure them across other companies' apps. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the IDFA is not used, ads are not personalised, and every feature keeps working. You can change this at any time in *iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking*. Nightpage Pro removes ads.
  • No account, no server of our own. Your books, the pages you scan, the text recognised from them and your reading position are stored only on your device. Text recognition and read-aloud run on your device; your books are never uploaded.
  • We never sell your data.

2. What stays on your device

  • The PDFs you add to your library. They are copied into the app's own storage on your device.
  • The pages you scan with the camera, the text recognised from them, and the books made from them. Text recognition (Apple Vision) runs on your device; your pages are never uploaded.
  • Read-aloud uses the voices built into iOS and runs on your device. The text being read is never sent anywhere.
  • Your reading position, page theme, brightness, voice and speed settings.
  • Delete a book in the Library (touch and hold its cover → Delete), or delete the app to remove everything.

What we do not collect:

  • Your name, email address or any account — the app has no sign-in.
  • The titles or contents of your books, or the pages you scan.
  • Your photo library — the app does not ask for access to it.
  • Your precise location, contacts, microphone or browsing history.

3. Device permissions

Nightpage: PDF Book Reader asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it. You can decline, and you can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings.

  • Camera — to scan pages of your paper books. Scans and the text recognised from them stay on your device.
  • Tracking (App Tracking Transparency) — to ask whether ads may use your advertising identifier. Saying no does not limit any feature.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with these service providers, each of which is bound by its own privacy policy and data-protection terms:

  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform) — Shows the ads that keep the free version free, measures how they perform, and asks for your ad-privacy choices where the law requires it. Receives: your advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking, your IP address, device model, OS version, and your interactions with ads (views and taps). Their privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Firebase (Google Analytics for Firebase, Crashlytics) — Tells us which features are used — for example whether a book was opened, read aloud or scanned — and reports crashes. Usage events never include the titles or contents of your books. Receives: in-app usage events, device model, OS version, app-generated identifiers and crash logs. Their privacy policy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Legal requirements — we may disclose information if required by law, or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets.

5. How long we keep it

Data stored on your device stays there until you delete it or uninstall the app. We do not run our own servers and keep no copy of the content you submit. Advertising partners retain ad data only as described in their own policies. Analytics and crash data are kept for the provider's standard retention period.

6. Legal bases

For personalised ads we rely on your consent (the App Tracking Transparency prompt and, where the law requires it, the ad consent form); you can withdraw it at any time. Non-personalised ads rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the app free. For analytics and crash diagnostics we rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and improving it. For purchases, we rely on performing our contract with you.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal information. In the app you can:

  • Change your ad-privacy choices in *Settings → Privacy Choices* (shown in regions where the law requires it, such as the EEA, the UK and some US states).
  • Stop tracking in *iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking*.
  • Remove ads with Nightpage Pro.
  • Revoke Camera access in *iOS Settings → Nightpage*.
  • Delete your books in the Library, or delete the app to remove everything.

For any other request, email khoald2302.ai@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.
