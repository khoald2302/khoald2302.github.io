Privacy Policy

Sellkey · Last updated November 15, 2026

This policy explains what information Sellkey (“the app”, “we”) collects, why, and who it is shared with. It is written to match exactly what the app does.

Summary

  • No ads, no tracking. We do not show third-party ads, do not collect your advertising identifier (IDFA), and do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No account, no server of our own. Your keys, folders, images, files, clipboard history and settings are stored only on your iPhone. The Sellkey keyboard has no network access. The only data that leaves your iPhone is anonymous usage counting from the main app, which never includes anything you type, save or paste.
  • We never sell your data.

2. What stays on your device

  • Your keys (text, price quotes, images, files, bank-transfer QR details), folders, pins and recently used keys.
  • Clipboard history saved by the keyboard (only when you turn on Full Access), which expires after the time you choose in Settings.
  • Your "Me" profile details and settings.
  • Backups go only to a file or folder you choose (for example in Files or iCloud Drive); we never receive them.
  • Delete everything at any time by deleting the app.

What we do not collect:

  • Your name, email address or any account, because Sellkey has no sign-in.
  • Anything you type with the Sellkey keyboard, and the content of your keys, clipboard or pasted items — none of it leaves your iPhone.
  • Your advertising identifier (IDFA), precise location, contacts or browsing history. Sellkey does not track you across apps and never shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • Crash reports, because the app includes no crash-reporting SDK.
  • Payment details — Sellkey Pro is bought through Apple, and we never see your card, Apple Account or name.

3. Device permissions

Sellkey asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it. You can decline, and you can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings.

  • Keyboard Full Access (optional) — lets the keyboard copy images and files for you to paste, keep your clipboard history and share usage order with the app. Everything stays on your iPhone and the keyboard has no network access. Text keys work without Full Access.
  • Face ID — only to reveal keys you have marked as hidden. Face ID data is handled by iOS and never reaches the app.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal information and do not share it with advertisers. We share data only with these service providers, each of which is bound by its own privacy policy and data-protection terms:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase — Counts how the main app is used (for example whether setup was finished or a feature was opened) so we can fix problems and improve Sellkey. Not used for advertising or cross-app tracking. The keyboard itself contains no analytics. Receives: event names and counts (never the content of your keys, clipboard or anything you type), an app-instance identifier, app version, device model, OS version, locale, approximate region derived from your IP address. Their privacy policy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Apple (App Store purchases) — Processes the one-time Sellkey Pro purchase and verifies that you own it. Receives: your purchase receipt, handled by Apple. We never see your payment details, Apple Account or name.. Their privacy policy: www.apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Legal requirements — we may disclose information if required by law, or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets.

5. How long we keep it

Data stored on your device stays there until you delete it or uninstall the app. We do not run our own servers and keep no copy of the content you submit. Analytics and crash data are kept for the provider's standard retention period.

6. Legal bases

For analytics and crash diagnostics we rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and improving it. For purchases, we rely on performing our contract with you.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal information. In the app you can:

  • Turn Full Access on or off in iOS Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Sellkey. Text keys keep working without it.
  • Clear clipboard history and keyboard activity in Settings → What the keyboard did.
  • Delete any key; deleted keys stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days, then are removed for good.
  • Delete all your data by deleting the app.

For any other request, email khoald2302.ai@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Tracking

We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. We do not access your advertising identifier (IDFA), so the app never shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version, and material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
