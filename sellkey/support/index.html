Frequently asked questions

How do I turn on the Sellkey keyboard?
Open iOS Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard and choose Sellkey. Then, in any app, hold the globe key and pick Sellkey. The setup screen in the app walks you through it and lets you try a key.
Do I need Full Access?
No. Text keys, fill-in fields, variables and price quotes work without it. Full Access is only needed to paste images and files, keep a clipboard history and learn which keys you use most. Even with Full Access the keyboard has no network access and your data stays on your iPhone.
Why can't I see Sellkey in some text fields?
iOS switches to the system keyboard in password fields and some secure fields, and some apps block third-party keyboards. Tap the field again or switch back with the globe key.
Is Sellkey free? What does Sellkey Pro add?
Sellkey is free with up to 12 keys, no ads. Sellkey Pro is a one-time purchase — no subscription — that adds unlimited keys, Paste Stack and bank-transfer QR keys. Keys you already have always stay viewable, pasteable and exportable.
How do I restore Sellkey Pro?
Tap Restore Purchases on the Sellkey Pro screen. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy.
How do I move my keys to a new iPhone?
In Settings → Backup, export a backup file and import it on the new iPhone, or turn on automatic backup to a folder you choose.
How do I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
