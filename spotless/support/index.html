Frequently asked questions

I paid but the app still shows the upgrade screen.
Tap Restore Purchases (on the Premium screen, or in Settings → Restore Purchases). Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy.
How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are billed by Apple, so you cancel them in your Apple Account, not in the app: open iOS Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. See Apple's instructions.
How do I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
I deleted a photo by mistake.
Open the Photos app → Albums → Recently Deleted. iOS keeps deleted items there for 30 days.
The app finds no photos or only some of them.
Open iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Spotless and choose Full Access. With limited access the app can only scan the photos you selected.
Are my photos uploaded anywhere?
No. Every scan runs on your iPhone. Photos, videos, contacts and calendar events never leave your device.
How do I delete my data?
Delete the app. Your data lives only on your device, so this removes it completely.
How is my data handled?
See our Privacy Policy.
