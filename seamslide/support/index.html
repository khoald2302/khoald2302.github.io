Frequently asked questions

I paid but the app still shows the upgrade screen.
Tap Restore Purchases (in Settings → Restore Purchases, and at the bottom of the Seamslide Pro screen). Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy.
How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are billed by Apple, so you cancel them in your Apple Account, not in the app: open iOS Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. See Apple's instructions.
How do I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
Are my photos uploaded anywhere?
No. Editing, background removal and export all run on your iPhone. Your carousels and photos stay on your device.
Why do my slides look sharp in Seamslide but soft on Instagram?
Use Settings → Export Quality → Standard. It exports at exactly 1080 pixels wide, the size Instagram shows, so Instagram does not resize your photos again.
In which order should I post my slides?
Seamslide saves your slides to Photos in order, slide 1 first. In Instagram, select them in the same order. For profile grids, post the tile marked 1 first — it is the bottom-right tile.
Is there a free trial?
No, and you will never be charged unless you buy. The free version lets you make and export carousels of up to 20 slides, with a small Seamslide mark on the last slide.
What does Seamslide Pro include?
No watermark, all templates and fonts, background removal, video export, Brand Kit, and 3×2 and 3×3 profile grids. Choose monthly, yearly or a one-time Lifetime purchase. Yearly and Lifetime work with Family Sharing.
How do I restore my purchase on a new device?
Open *Settings → Restore Purchases*, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought with.
How do I delete my data?
Delete a carousel in Projects, or delete the app. Everything lives only on your iPhone, so this removes it completely.
