Privacy Policy

Seamslide: Carousel & Collage · Last updated October 23, 2026

This policy explains what information Seamslide: Carousel & Collage (“the app”, “we”) collects, why, and who it is shared with. It is written to match exactly what the app does.

Summary

  • No ads, no tracking. We do not show third-party ads, do not collect your advertising identifier (IDFA), and do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No account, no server of our own. Your carousels, the photos you add to them, background cut-outs and your Brand Kit are stored only on your iPhone. Editing, background removal and export all run on your device; your photos are never uploaded.
  • We never sell your data.

2. What stays on your device

  • Your carousels and the photos you add to them. Photos you pick with Apple's photo picker are copied into the app's own storage on your iPhone, resized to at most 4096 pixels.
  • Background cut-outs. Remove Background uses Apple Vision on your iPhone; the photo never leaves your device.
  • Your Brand Kit — colours, fonts and logo.
  • Your settings — export quality, snapping and whether you have seen the introduction.
  • Delete a carousel in Projects (touch and hold it → Delete), or delete the app to remove everything.

What we do not collect:

  • Your name, email address or any account — the app has no sign-in.
  • Your photos, the text you add or anything you design.
  • Your photo library — Seamslide never browses it. You choose photos with Apple's photo picker, and the app only asks to add finished slides, grid tiles and videos to your library.
  • Your advertising identifier (IDFA), precise location, contacts, microphone or browsing history. Seamslide shows no ads, does not track you across apps and never shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt.

3. Device permissions

Seamslide: Carousel & Collage asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it. You can decline, and you can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings.

  • Photos (add only) — to save your finished slides, grid tiles and videos to your photo library, in order. Seamslide cannot read your library with this permission.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal information and do not share it with advertisers. We share data only with these service providers, each of which is bound by its own privacy policy and data-protection terms:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase — Measures how the app is used (for example whether a carousel was created, exported or saved as a video, whether the Pro screen was opened and a purchase completed, and which errors people run into) so we can fix problems and improve Seamslide. Not used for advertising or cross-app tracking. Receives: a small set of usage events and their settings (never your photos, your text or anything you design), an app-instance identifier, app version, device model, OS version, locale, approximate region derived from your IP address, and purchase events (product and result). Their privacy policy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Apple (App Store purchases) — Processes your purchase or subscription payment and verifies that you own Seamslide Pro. Receives: your purchase receipt, handled by Apple. We never see your payment details, Apple Account or name.. Their privacy policy: www.apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Legal requirements — we may disclose information if required by law, or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets.

5. How long we keep it

Data stored on your device stays there until you delete it or uninstall the app. We do not run our own servers and keep no copy of the content you submit. Analytics and crash data are kept for the provider's standard retention period.

6. Legal bases

For analytics and crash diagnostics we rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and improving it. For purchases, we rely on performing our contract with you.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal information. In the app you can:

  • Revoke Photos access in *iOS Settings → Seamslide*. You can still share slides with the share button.
  • Delete your data by deleting carousels in Projects, or by deleting the app — everything Seamslide stored is on your iPhone.
  • Cancel a subscription in *iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions*, or from Manage Subscription in Seamslide Settings.

For any other request, email khoald2302.ai@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Tracking

We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. We do not access your advertising identifier (IDFA), so the app never shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version, and material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
